No One Noticed

2026 · Director/Editor

This short film captures the bittersweet intersection of distance and devotion.

Drawing inspiration from my friend's narratives and the melancholic tones of "No One Noticed," I independently took on roles of director, cinematographer, and editor. By streamlining production into a highly efficient 1.5-hour shoot, I captured the intimate vulnerability inherent in long-distance relationships.
