Design

马到成功 一马当先 “Succeed in Year of the Horse” [2025]

2025 · Adobe Illustrator

Inspiration: Why Chinese?

Chinese aesthetics have been quite intimidating to me - every pattern and shape carries so much weight from the past that I hesitated to attempt integrating it into my own work. I was afraid I wasn't qualified to use Chinese motifs as it might not be fully representational of historical context.

It wasn't until my Chinese social media feed started pushing 新中式 (modern Chinese aesthetics) where I felt qualified to integrate certain elements into my designs.

Inspiration: Why a horse calendar?

New years is a big deal in China, particularly Chinese New Year. It seemed like a good first step to take into experimenting with 新中式 (modern Chinese aesthetics).

Regardless of which New Years it is, friends and family will flood your messages with greetings and wishes with four-character idioms - such as the two on right and left of the poster.

Other design choices

I made an effort to use a lot of shapes that I would see in Chinese architecture. They are often symmetric, balanced shapes that translated a sense of peace and stability to me. ​

As for the horses, it is because the year 2026 is the Year of the Horse according to Chinese Zodiac Calendar.

​ ​​For the color choice, I wanted it to be bright, just like how I remember New Years celebration to be like in China, but I didn't want to use the classic red. An alternative is a not quite traditionally Chinese color - hot pink.

I only started listening to Manchild by Sabrina Carpenter after watching a compilation of Dating in 2025 on TikTok. This bridge was so catchy and I think reflective of a lot of modern women's experiences with men. ​ I created this banner as a playful reflection of the current heterosexual dating scene.
